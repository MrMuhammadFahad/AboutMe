    Pose-based gait analysis

    PurposeTurns a walking video into measurements that describe how a person moves.

    How I built itMediaPipe Pose finds hip, knee and ankle points in the video. The pipeline uses those points to calculate joint angles, step duration, cadence and ankle movement.

    Why it mattersThe eight measurements turn visual movement into structured data that can be compared or passed to a machine-learning model. This connects computer vision to a practical input for gait analysis.
